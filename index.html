A free quiz game that helps 2nd-year IT students review HTML lessons from CIT16233Z with instant feedback, a countdown timer, and saved best scores.

  • 3 Topics
  • 10 Questions each
  • 20s Per question

Pick a Topic

Choose a Quiz Topic

Select a topic, answer 10 multiple-choice questions before the timer runs out, and try to beat your best score.

How to Play

  1. Pick one of the three topics on the home screen.
  2. Read each question and click your answer before the countdown ends.
  3. Get instant right or wrong feedback after every answer.
  4. See your final score and best score, then retry or go back home.

Tip: Your best score for each topic is saved in this browser, so you can keep improving over time.
