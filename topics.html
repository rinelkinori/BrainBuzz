Quick Reference: Basic HTML Structure

The document structure you will be asked about in the quizzes.

  <head>
    <meta charset="UTF-8">
    <meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0">
    <title>My Page</title>
  </head>
  <body>
    <h1>Hello, world!</h1>
  </body>
</html>
